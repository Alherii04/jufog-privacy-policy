# Privacy Policy for Jufog Assistant

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

## Introduction

Jufog Assistant ("we", "our", or "the Service") is a WhatsApp-based business assistant used by Jufog to process invoices, track payments, manage customer transactions, and support sales operations.

By interacting with Jufog Assistant, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

When you interact with Jufog Assistant, we may collect:

- Your WhatsApp phone number
- Your WhatsApp display name
- Messages you send to the assistant
- Documents and files you submit, including invoices and payment receipts
- Transaction and invoice information related to your business activities
- Technical information provided by WhatsApp Business Platform

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Process invoices and payment records
- Generate customer invoices
- Match payments to outstanding invoices
- Support sales representatives and administrators
- Maintain accounting and transaction records
- Improve the functionality and reliability of the Service
- Respond to support requests and operational issues

## Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only:

- With authorized Jufog personnel for business operations
- With service providers required to operate the system, such as WhatsApp Business Platform, Meta, Supabase, and cloud hosting providers
- When required by law or regulatory authorities

## Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Business records, invoices, payment allocations, and related information may be stored securely in databases and cloud infrastructure used by Jufog.

## Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for operational, accounting, legal, and business purposes.

Information may be deleted when it is no longer required for these purposes.

## Your Rights

You may request:

- Access to information associated with you
- Correction of inaccurate information
- Deletion of your information where applicable

Requests may be submitted using the contact details below.

Your use of WhatsApp remains subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

Jufog Assistant is intended for business use and is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law.
